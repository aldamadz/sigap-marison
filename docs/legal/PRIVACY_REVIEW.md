  subject rights, processing, controller/processor duties and transfer. This
  is not an independent legal compliance audit or certification.
- No invented entity, email/address, fixed retention period, residency,
  encryption-at-rest guarantee, transfer approval or retroactive consent.
  Requests go through existing official IT/admin/HRD channels. The company
  should designate a directly published privacy contact and validate its
  provider/retention register with its responsible legal/privacy owner.
